## The referral link: guaranteed credit with a random amount

The closest thing to a working whatnot promo code is Whatnot's buyer referral program. You sign up through an invite link, and once the signup steps are complete, bonus credit lands in your account automatically. No code typing involved.

The amount is randomized. Whatnot's own referral program terms say new users receive credit in their local currency after completing the steps. In the UK, users have reported anywhere from £10 to £200, with £10 as the guaranteed floor. In Europe, the invite screen shows a €10–€200 range, but the amount most often revealed is €15. US reports center on a $10 first-order bonus. Plan around the minimum, treat anything above it as a nice surprise.

The signup flow is short:

1. Open the invite link
2. Create a brand-new account (existing accounts don't qualify)
3. Verify your phone number
4. Download the iOS or Android app
5. Add a valid payment card

The credit then applies automatically at checkout. A few rules from the fine print worth knowing before you build a cart around it:

- It covers the item subtotal only. Shipping, taxes, and processing fees come out of your pocket.
- No change is given. A $10 credit on a $7 item wastes $3, so shop to at least the credit's value.
- It expires 90 days after being issued.
- It only works with sellers operating in the same currency, and it doesn't apply to offers or Free Pickup orders.
- One credit per person. Creating multiple accounts to farm the bonus can get the credit canceled and accounts suspended.

## The no-referral path: up to $20 coupon plus free shipping

Here's the tradeoff that most coupon pages never mention: Whatnot runs a separate first-time buyer program, and its eligibility rules conflict with the referral credit.

The First-Time Buyer Coupon is offered directly by Whatnot to new users in the US, UK, France, Germany, Australia, and Canada. Each coupon is worth up to $20 USD or the local equivalent. You don't get it automatically — you have to enter an eligible livestream and tap the Free Gift prompt, or open the gift in the Rewards Hub. Once claimed, it auto-applies to your first eligible livestream purchase.

Three restrictions matter:

- The coupon expires **7 days** after you claim the gift. If you complete a purchase before claiming it, the gift and coupon can expire immediately.
- Eligibility is generally limited to users who haven't received account credit through another promotion. In plain terms: if you signed up through a referral link and got referral credit, you're typically locked out of this coupon.
- Not every user is offered a Free Gift — Whatnot reserves the right to decide eligibility.

US buyers who skip the referral link also qualify for free shipping on their first purchase: up to $25, applied automatically at checkout with no code. If shipping runs over $25, you get the $25 credit and pay the rest. Unused, it expires 60 days after signup. And the same conflict applies — Whatnot's help documentation states that buyers who sign up using a referral link are not eligible for the first-time buyer free shipping incentive.

So the math is a real fork. A guaranteed $10 referral credit versus a possible $20 coupon plus up to $25 in shipping credit. If your first order is small and shipping-heavy, the no-referral path can easily be worth more. If you're planning to spend well beyond $10 anyway, the referral credit is the sure thing.

## Seller coupons: where an actual code gets typed

Real, typeable Whatnot promo codes do exist — sellers create them for their own live shows. These can be fixed amounts or percentage discounts, sometimes restricted to first-time buyers of that seller, and they're only valid on qualifying purchases inside that seller's show.

Redeeming one takes four taps in the app: join the qualifying seller's live, open the **Wallet** icon, enter the code in the **Coupon Code** field, and tap **Apply**. Codes are 4–15 characters, capital letters and numbers only. Once claimed, the active coupon appears under your shipping and payment information.

Two mechanics worth internalizing. First, stacking order: Whatnot applies the seller coupon before referral credit. On a $40 item subtotal with a $10 seller coupon and $15 referral credit, you'd pay $15 on the subtotal — with shipping, tax, and fees still added on top. A "$25 off" headline doesn't make a $40 order cost $15. Second, the no-change rule applies to seller coupons too, so keep your qualifying cart at or above the coupon's face value.

The easiest way to find these codes is simply watching the shows you'd buy from anyway. Sellers drop limited-time and limited-quantity codes during broadcasts, and bigger promotions cluster around major shopping events.

## FAQ

**Is there an official Whatnot promo code for new users?**
No. Whatnot has no sitewide code. New-user value comes from referral link credit, the first-time buyer coupon, and (in the US) the free shipping perk — each with its own eligibility rules.

**Do the first-time buyer coupon and referral credit stack?**
Generally no. The coupon's terms limit eligibility to users who haven't received account credit through another promotion, and referral-link signups are explicitly excluded from the first-time buyer free shipping incentive. Pick one path at signup.

**Can I use seller coupons and referral credit together?**
Yes. Whatnot applies the seller coupon first, then the referral credit, with shipping, tax, and fees calculated on the remainder.

**Why didn't my cashback track?**

**How long do I have to use my referral credit?**
Referral credits typically expire 90 days after issuance. First-time buyer coupons are much tighter — 7 days from the moment you claim the Free Gift.
